07 / Good to know

A few answers.

Have another question? Reach out directly and we’ll help you figure out the details.

Ask us anything
How do I check availability?

Send your preferred date, location and type of shoot through the enquiry form or WhatsApp. We’ll confirm availability and talk through next steps.

Can a package be tailored?

Yes. The collections are starting points. Share your plans and we can discuss the coverage, location and options that suit your day.

Do you travel for shoots?

Travel can be discussed when we plan your shoot. Include the location in your enquiry so we can confirm any arrangements and costs.

How does transport work for PA system hire?

PA system hire is K3,500. Transport to and from your event is provided by the person booking the system. Please confirm availability and setup needs with GHOP.

How do I secure a booking?

Once we’ve confirmed availability and agreed on the details, GHOP will explain the booking and payment steps for your selected service.

Can I update the prices on this page?

Use the “Edit prices” button in the Collections section. In this demo, the updated amounts are saved in this browser only; a shared live website needs a connected content manager or database.
