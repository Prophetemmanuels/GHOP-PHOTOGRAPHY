03 / Photoshop-ready workflow

Match the plate.
Make it believable.

Download the clean scene, isolate the client, and make the light, camera angle and floor line work together.

01 / Choose

Find an empty scene

Pick a background with clear floor/foreground space and no people, models or distracting subjects.

02 / Check

Match the camera

Look for a similar camera height, lens perspective, horizon and floor angle to your client's portrait.

03 / Composite

Place the subject

Remove the original background, refine hair and edges, then scale and position the subject naturally.

04 / Finish

Match light & shadow

Match colour temperature, contrast and direction of light. Add a soft contact shadow so the subject sits in the scene.

  • Prefer high-resolution files; aim for 3000 px or more on the long edge when available.
  • Some specialist freebies are smaller; check pixel dimensions before starting the edit.
  • Check the source license for commercial/client use before downloading or publishing.
  • Keep a clean original plate and save your Photoshop composite as a separate layered file.
